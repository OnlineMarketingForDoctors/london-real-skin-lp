Is it right for you

Made for people who already put the work in

Body sculpting is about tone and definition, not weight loss. It suits you best if you are close to your goal weight and want results that training alone has not delivered.

The plateaued trainer Past the point training reaches.
  • You train 3–5 times a week but the mirror has stopped changing
  • You want visible core and limb definition
  • You want evidence, and a measured, medically supervised plan
The time-poor professional Thirty minutes. No downtime.
  • You can’t take time off for surgical recovery
  • You’d book around a lunch break or commute
  • You want one clear plan from a clinic you can trust

You may be a good candidate if

  • You are near your goal weight
  • You have stubborn fat that resists exercise
  • You want firmer, more defined muscle
  • You want to rebuild core strength
  • You prefer a non-surgical option
  • You are happy to keep a healthy lifestyle

When it isn’t suitable

We won’t treat you if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a pacemaker or metal or electronic implants near the treatment area, or have certain medical conditions. We check your full medical history at your free consultation to keep treatment safe.

Frequently asked questions

Ask us anything

Both use HIFEM energy to build and tone muscle. EMSCULPT NEO adds synchronised radiofrequency to reduce fat in the same session (Sculpt + Burn), and treats the abdomen, flanks, buttocks, arms, thighs and calves. EMBODY focuses on toning and strengthening the abdomen and buttocks with HIFEM (Sculpt + Tone). We recommend the right one, or a combination, at your consultation.

An applicator is strapped over the treatment area. HIFEM energy causes thousands of supramaximal muscle contractions, which the muscle adapts to by building density and volume. At the same time, radiofrequency gently heats the fat layer, damaging fat cells so the body can clear them naturally over the following weeks.

No anaesthetic is needed. You will feel warmth and strong muscle contractions, which many patients compare to an intense workout or a deep massage. The intensity is built up gradually and adjusted to your comfort throughout.

For EMSCULPT NEO we generally recommend a course of four sessions, spaced five to ten days apart. EMBODY is usually a course of at least four sessions, two to three days apart. Your practitioner will give you a clear, realistic plan at your consultation.

Many patients feel a difference straight away, with visible results typically two to four weeks after the final session and continuing to improve for several weeks after that. Because the treatment builds lean muscle, results are long-lasting when combined with a healthy lifestyle. Top-up sessions can help maintain them.

No. You can return to work, the gym and normal activities straight after your session. Some patients notice mild muscle soreness for a day or two, much like after a hard workout.

Body sculpting is not a weight-loss treatment. It is designed to reduce stubborn fat and build muscle in specific areas, so it works best for people who are close to their goal weight and want better tone and definition.

EMSCULPT NEO and EMBODY are non-invasive and FDA-cleared, and treatment at London Real Skin is carried out in a dermatology-led, CQC-regulated clinic. We review your medical history first; treatment is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or with a pacemaker or metal implants near the area.
