How BBL HERO works

Light that targets what ageing leaves behind

BBL HERO (BroadBand Light) delivers short, precise pulses of light into the skin. That light is absorbed by the pigment and the tiny blood vessels that cause brown spots and redness, where it converts to gentle heat.

  1. 1
    Targets pigment & rednessLight energy is absorbed by excess melanin and dilated vessels, the root of sun damage and rosacea.
  2. 2
    Clears and evens toneTreated pigment rises to the surface and sheds over the following days, revealing clearer skin beneath.
  3. 3
    Stimulates healthy skinThe warmth prompts the skin's natural renewal, so tone and texture keep improving over your course.

The treatment

What happens during your treatment

After your skin is cleansed, you and your practitioner wear protective eyewear and a cool gel is applied. With BBL HERO, the smooth handpiece glides over the skin, delivering brief pulses of light that feel like a warm, light snap. With MOXI, the fractional laser is gently passed across the skin, feeling warm with a mild prickle, and when the two are combined they are carried out in the same appointment. Numbing cream is not usually needed and a typical face takes around 15 to 30 minutes. Afterwards the skin may look a little pink or feel warm, like a mild sunburn; treated pigment can darken briefly and flake away over a few days, and MOXI can leave a fine, sandpaper-like texture, revealing fresher, clearer skin beneath.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

BBL HERO stands for BroadBand Light, High Energy Rapid Output. It is the latest, most powerful version of Sciton's BroadBand Light platform, an advanced form of intense pulsed light (IPL). It delivers precise light energy to target pigment and redness, treating sun damage, brown spots, rosacea and uneven tone quickly and comfortably.

Sun damage and pigmentation, brown and age spots, freckling, facial redness, rosacea, broken capillaries and other vascular marks, uneven tone, dullness and the early signs of ageing. It can be used on the face, neck, chest, hands and most areas of the body.

Most patients find BBL HERO very comfortable. Each pulse feels like a warm, light snap against the skin, and the handpiece helps keep you cool throughout. Numbing cream is not usually required. You and your practitioner will wear protective eyewear during the treatment.

Most patients have between 2 and 4 sessions for the best results, usually spaced a few weeks apart. Your practitioner will give you a clear, realistic plan at your consultation, and many patients then choose occasional maintenance treatments to keep their results.

Downtime is minimal. Straight after treatment the skin can look slightly pink or feel warm, like mild sunburn, usually settling within a few hours to a day. Treated brown spots often darken briefly, then flake away over the next few days to reveal clearer skin. Most people return to normal activities right away.

Many patients notice brighter, more even skin within one to two weeks of their first session, as treated pigment clears. Results continue to build through your course, and are best maintained by protecting your skin from the sun with a daily SPF.

Light and laser treatments work best on untanned skin, so we usually advise avoiding sun exposure and self-tan before and after treatment. Your practitioner will advise on the right timing for your skin and lifestyle at your consultation.

MOXI is a gentle, non-ablative 1927nm fractional laser. It creates tiny, controlled micro-zones in the skin that trigger its natural repair process, boosting collagen and renewing the surface. This improves texture, fine lines, pigmentation and dullness with minimal downtime, and it is suitable for all skin types and can be used year round.

BBL HERO uses broadband light to target pigment and the blood vessels behind brown spots and redness, so it is ideal for sun damage, pigmentation and rosacea. MOXI is a fractional laser that resurfaces and renews the skin's texture and tone. They work in different ways, which is exactly why combining them gives such a complete result.
